# Interpretation of supplementary S19

The contribution remains empirical evaluation of source-only warnings for existing classifiers. CTU adds a small capture-level check in another representation. Original four-dataset, temporal and family-exclusion results remain unchanged.

Descriptive capture-mean rank correlations are -0.10, -0.30 and -0.80 for sampling instability, environmental instability and gap. They do not establish general association, a reverse warning or significance. Fixed ridge models fail to improve the intercept baseline under both split schemes. LR is the only classifier, so model identity is constant; the baseline differs from the original three-model categorical baseline. Mean RMSE averages source-specific RMSEs rather than pooling squared errors.

Role checks were executed on input arrays in Colab; compact reports omit those arrays. Independent review reconstructs numerical results but does not replay raw recovery or feature-based predictions. Support gates are not proof of power or independence. Five sources and three provider-linked specimen folds are from one institution.

Broader independent-site validation, measured production prevalence and semantically certified fine-family warning utility remain unestablished. Do not relabel the completed bounded pilot as completing those broader goals.
